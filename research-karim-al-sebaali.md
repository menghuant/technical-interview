## The Sebaaly (Al Sebaali / Al Sebaaly) Family

### Family Name
- **Surname variants:** Sebaali, Sebaaly, Al Sebaali, Al Sebaaly
- **Arabic:** Possibly "سبعلي" (Sab'ali)
- **Global distribution:** ~365 people worldwide bear this surname (relatively rare)
- **Primary concentrations:** Saudi Arabia (278), United States (35), Canada (18), Qatar (16)
- **Geographic origin:** Primarily West Asia / Arabian Peninsula (84%)
- **Phonetic variants:** Sebaly, Sebaale

### Geographic Roots in Lebanon
The Sebaaly family is rooted in the **Metn District (Mount Lebanon Governorate)**, specifically in and around:
- **Bikfaya** - A town in the Matn district known for its historical significance
- **Mayassa** - A village in North Metn
- **Shwayya** - Another village in North Metn
- **Antoura / Kesrouane area** (where Karim attended school)

These are predominantly **Christian (Maronite)** areas in Mount Lebanon.

### Al Sebaaly Association
There is a formal **Al Sebaaly Association** (Facebook: @sebaaliassociation), described as a non-profit organization with the objective to reunite the "Al Sebaaly" family and to offer help and services to all family members. The existence of a formal family association suggests a sizable and organized clan.

### Family Business Interests

The Sebaaly family has multiple established businesses in the Bikfaya/Metn area:

1. **Sebaali Trading** (est. 1967)
   - Founded by **Mr. Jamal Sebaali and his brothers**
   - Located on Bikfaya Main Road
   - Sells fireplaces, stoves, barbecues, agriculture equipment, paints, waterproofing, and accessories
   - Website: sebaalitrading.com
   - Registered as **SEBAALI TRADING CO S.A.R.L.**

2. **Business Services Office (BSO)** (est. 1988)
   - Founded by **Nabil Sebaaly** in Bikfaya, El Metn
   - Focus: Travel, Insurance, Recruitment, Event Management
   - Obtained IATA certification (May 1, 1991)
   - Expanded to Beirut (1996) and later to Dubai
   - Now a group with six branches
   - Website: bso.com.lb

3. **SEBTRA SARL**
   - Located in "Nabil Sebaaly building" on Main Street, Bikfaya
   - Appears to be part of the BSO group

4. **Bachir Assaad Sebaaly Trading**
   - Located in "Bachir Sebaaly property" on Main Street, Mayassa, El Metn
   - A separate family-run trading business
